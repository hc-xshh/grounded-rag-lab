# Subscription plans

Ledgerline is sold as a monthly subscription with three plans. All plans include
the full feature set; the differences are seat count, included API volume and
support tier.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Price | Seats included | Included API calls / month | Overage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $49 per month | 3 | 100,000 | $0.40 per 1,000 calls |
| Growth | $199 per month | 10 | 1,000,000 | $0.40 per 1,000 calls |
| Scale | $749 per month | Unlimited | 5,000,000 | $0.25 per 1,000 calls |

## Annual billing

Annual billing is available on every plan and gives two months free, which is a
16% discount compared with paying monthly. Annual subscriptions are invoiced up
front for the full term.

## Free trial

Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial. The trial includes Growth
plan limits and does not require a payment card. When the trial ends, the
workspace moves to read-only mode unless a plan is selected.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated to the day. Downgrades take
effect at the start of the next billing period so that no included volume is
lost mid-cycle.

## Seat changes

Seats can be added or removed at any time. Seat changes are prorated to the day
and appear on the next invoice. Removing a seat does not delete the user's
historical activity.
