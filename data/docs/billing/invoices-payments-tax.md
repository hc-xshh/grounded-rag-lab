# Invoices, payments and tax

## Invoice schedule

Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period and are due within
14 days. If a payment method is on file, it is charged automatically on day 15
when the invoice is still unpaid.

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, SEPA direct debit for EUR
invoices and ACH transfer for USD invoices. Bank wire is available for invoices
above $5,000 and must be requested before the invoice is issued.

## Proration

Plan upgrades and seat changes are prorated to the day. Credits from a downgrade
are applied to the next invoice rather than refunded.

## Refunds

Monthly subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of the charge. Once
a refund is issued for a monthly plan, the workspace is cancelled at the end of
the current period.

Annual subscriptions are non-refundable after the first 14 days. Before that
window closes, the unused portion is refunded minus the value of any month
already used.

## Failed payments

When a charge fails we retry on day 1, day 3 and day 7 after the failure. If the
invoice is still unpaid on day 14 the workspace is paused. Paused workspaces are
kept for 60 days and can be restored by paying the outstanding invoice, after
which the data is deleted.

## Tax

VAT is collected for customers in the European Union and the United Kingdom. GST
is collected in Australia, India and Singapore. Business customers outside those
regions can apply the reverse charge rule by adding a valid VAT or GST
identifier to the billing profile. Tax identifiers are validated at the moment
they are saved, not at invoice time.
