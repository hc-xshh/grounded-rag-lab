# Support severity levels and escalation

## Support hours

Standard support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 UTC. Severity 1
incidents are handled 24 hours a day, every day, including public holidays.

## Severity definitions

- **P1 - Critical**: the workspace is unavailable or invoices cannot be issued at
  all. No workaround exists.
- **P2 - High**: a major feature is degraded, for example payments are accepted
  but not reflected in reports. A workaround may exist.
- **P3 - Normal**: a single feature behaves incorrectly but business continues.
- **P4 - Low**: cosmetic issues, documentation questions and feature requests.

## Response targets

| Severity | First response target |
| --- | --- |
| P1 | 1 hour, 24/7 |
| P2 | 4 hours during support hours |
| P3 | 1 business day |
| P4 | 3 business days |

These are response targets, not resolution targets. Resolution time depends on
the root cause and is communicated in the incident thread.

## Escalation

P1 incidents are escalated to the on-call engineer automatically if no first
response is recorded within 2 hours. Any customer can request escalation to a
duty manager; requests are acknowledged within 30 minutes during support hours.

## Status page and incident communication

The status page is updated within 15 minutes of a confirmed P1 and then at least
every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved. Post-incident reviews are
published within 5 business days for P1 and P2 incidents.

## Plan-specific support

Scale plan customers are assigned a named technical account manager who attends
quarterly reviews. Growth plan customers can request a shared Slack channel at
no extra cost.
